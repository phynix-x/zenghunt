How to judge whether a headphone deal is actually good

A big discount number is only the beginning.

Start with the real selling price

Compare the current selling price with recent prices when possible. A high MRP followed by a large discount can look impressive without being the best market price.

Check the features you actually need

For wireless headphones, consider battery life, comfort, microphone quality, connectivity, codec support and warranty rather than choosing only by discount percentage.

Read ratings in context

A rating is more useful when paired with a meaningful number of reviews. Look for repeated complaints about comfort, battery, durability or connectivity.

Check seller, warranty and returns

Before paying, verify who is selling the item, what warranty applies and what the return or replacement conditions are.
