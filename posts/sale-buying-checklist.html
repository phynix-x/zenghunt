SMART SHOPPING

5 checks to make before buying during a sale

1. Compare the price

Check the current price against other reputable retailers and, when available, recent price history.

2. Check the exact model

Confirm model number, storage, size, colour and included accessories. Similar-looking variants can have very different prices.

3. Check the seller

Review seller information, ratings and fulfilment details where available.

4. Read warranty and return terms

Make sure the deal still makes sense if you need a replacement or return.

5. Ignore artificial urgency

A countdown or “limited stock” message should not replace your normal checks. Buy when the product is right for you.
