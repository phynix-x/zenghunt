About ZenGhunt

ZenGhunt is being built as a multi-store deal discovery and comparison platform.

The target capability is search, filters, store comparison, price history, deal scoring and direct store links using original ZenGhunt code and design.

Live-store roadmap

Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Meesho, AJIO, Tata CLiQ and additional stores will be connected through approved APIs, affiliate feeds or other permitted data sources.
