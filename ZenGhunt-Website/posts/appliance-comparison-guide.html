How to compare appliances before clicking Buy Now

Compare the specification that affects your use

For each appliance, identify the few specifications that directly affect performance and capacity. More numbers do not automatically mean a better product.

Consider running costs

Energy use, consumables and maintenance can matter more than a small difference in purchase price over the product's lifetime.

Check service and warranty

Verify warranty coverage and the availability of service support in your area before choosing between similar products.

Look beyond the headline discount

Compare the final payable price, included accessories and delivery conditions before deciding which offer is better.
