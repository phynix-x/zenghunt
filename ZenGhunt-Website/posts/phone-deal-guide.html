PHONE BUYING

How to evaluate a smartphone deal

Compare the exact model, storage, warranty, camera needs, battery and total price before treating a phone discount as a bargain.

Check the exact product

Always confirm the model, variant and included items. Product names can be very similar while specifications differ.

Compare the total value

Look at the final payable price together with warranty, returns, delivery and any required accessories.

Make the decision calmly

A good deal is one that gives you useful value at a price you are comfortable paying. Verify the final details on the retailer's website before purchase.
