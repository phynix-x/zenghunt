ABOUT

Why ZenGhunt?

ZenGhunt is a shopping discovery project focused on helping Indian shoppers make more informed purchase decisions.

Our approach

We look at factors such as discount, product information, ratings and review volume when evaluating potential deals. A large percentage discount alone does not automatically make a product a good buy.

What we publish

Our site combines practical buying guides with selected offers. Product prices, stock, ratings and retailer terms can change at any time, so shoppers should verify details before purchasing.

Contact

For website feedback or corrections, contact the ZenGhunt team through the contact method associated with this website.
